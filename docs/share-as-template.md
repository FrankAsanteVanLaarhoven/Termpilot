You must do this in the **Grok Bot app** on your machine (same flow as Matt Palmer’s Loops bot). This session cannot log into LinkedIn or Grok Bot for you.

## A. Create the Bot and share the `x.ai/bot/...` link

1. Open **Grok Bot**.
2. **New** (or `Cmd+N`) → **Create new agent**.
3. Open **Bot actions → Edit Profile**.
4. Name: `TermPilot`  
   Title: `Verified control tower for student life`
5. Paste the whole of `grok/template/PROFILE-PASTE.txt` into the description.  
   File: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/FrankAsanteVanLaarhoven/Termpilot/main/grok/template/PROFILE-PASTE.txt
6. Save. Send the first task from the profile so the Bot has one successful run.
7. Open the Bot menu → **Share as template** (or **Copy share link**).
8. Confirm the preview has **no API keys, no student email, no MCP**.
9. Copy the public URL. Published TermPilot share:

**https://x.ai/bot/OeUkDHEV4ykNb2EDpUO1m**

10. Paste that URL into the LinkedIn post under “Shared Bot template”.

Anyone who opens it can **Add to Grok Bot**. That copies the recipe, not your computer or logins.
